THE LONG RIDE

ROADMAP

From the first starting gate to a community-led future, every phase is built to reward the riders who stay with us.

  1. 01

    PHASE 01

    COMMUNITY BUILDING

    Before the collection launch, we will run activities that grow our community and reward early supporters.

    • Whitelist applications will open.
    • Community contests and special events will be held.
    • Active members can earn whitelist access.
    • Collection and mint details will be announced.
  2. 02

    PHASE 02

    NFT MINT

    A total of 3,333 NFTs will be minted.

    • 3,000 NFTs are reserved for the community mint.
    • 333 NFTs are held in the team reserve and will never be sold.
    • All reserve NFTs will be distributed to the community through contests, giveaways, rewards and special events.
  3. 03

    PHASE 03

    TOKEN LAUNCH

    The project token will launch two days after the NFT mint is complete.

    • The official contract address will only be shared through verified project accounts.
    • The ecosystem will begin expanding alongside the launch.
    • Launch details and token economics will be shared transparently with the community.
  4. 04

    PHASE 04

    HOLDER AIRDROP

    NFT holders will qualify for the project's token airdrop.

    • A snapshot will be taken of eligible holder wallets.
    • Token amounts and distribution criteria will be announced later.
    • Airdrop details will be published through official channels.
    • The airdrop rewards genuine holders and long-term community members.
  5. 05

    PHASE 05

    COMMUNITY-DRIVEN FUTURE

    After mint and token launch, the project will keep growing with the community at its center.

    • Team-reserve NFTs will be distributed through regular events.
    • Holder-only contests and community activities will continue.
    • New partnerships and collection developments will be announced.
    • Community feedback will help shape future decisions.
